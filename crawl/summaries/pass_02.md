## 4. Observations

- **Phase-separation assays are a missing assay class, not just missing terms.** ALYREF's papers
  assay condensate formation by in vitro droplet imaging, FRAP and opto-droplets. The database has
  no entry for this and no function term for it, so none of this evidence can attach to a protein.
  Candidate terms T012, T013 and F001 go together.
- **Two cAMP biosensor formats are missing terms.** GloSensor (2 papers) and genetically encoded
  fluorescent sensors such as Pink Flamindo. The term table has "glosensor camp" only as a synonym
  of the cAMP function term, so the readout cannot be labelled. GPCR papers now use these in place
  of the HTRF cAMP kit that entry GPCR1 lists first.
- **Name collisions are the single largest loss: 19 of 145 papers in this batch, 13%.** Four kinds:
  - alias collision on a short uppercase synonym: UNC119's "RG4" (retinal protein 4) matched RNA
    G-quadruplex "rG4", which cost all 10 of its papers;
  - species collision on the same symbol: INHA matched *M. tuberculosis* InhA (enoyl-ACP reductase)
    in 8 of 10 papers, a different protein with its own large inhibitor literature;
  - alias collision with a protein family member: ACTC1's "ACTC" matched activin C;
  - alias collision with mutation notation: F13A1's "F13A" matched the peptide analogue
    apelin-13(F13A).
  A fix should drop aliases of 3 to 4 characters unless the gene symbol itself, require the species,
  and treat "<letter><number><letter>" patterns as mutation notation.
- **Expression-only papers are the largest honest loss.** SLC2A2, ZYX, DBN1 and MEIS1's leukaemia
  papers measure the protein's level, not its function. SLC2A2 had 10 papers and no assay of
  transport. This is not a defect to fix; it is what the literature holds for these targets.
- Technique-aware linking is working: ZFP91's MST result linked nothing, while its LC-MS/MS binding
  result linked G5 (mass spectrometry) and not the DSF, SPR or ITC entries.
- 8 of 15 targets with papers gained something; 7 of 20 had no paper pass the fetch gate at all
  (CD209, CTSF, KCTD11, MAPK13, SERPINA2, plus CPB1 and SERPINA6 from batch 0).
