## 4. Observations

- Six terms are supported by 2 or more papers and are ready to propose together: manual patch clamp,
  thallium flux, MST, HPLC/HPTLC product separation, coupled-enzyme metabolite detection, flow
  cytometry. Thallium flux already has an entry (IC2) but no technology term, so campaigns cannot be
  labelled with it.
- The most common new link is ENZ3 (cellular product or biomarker readout) for metabolic enzymes
  whose campaigns are all biochemical: ACLY, GLO1, SRD5A1, GSR, MGMT.
- RGS4 has no protein class, so its HTS, AlphaScreen and BRET assays cannot link to any entry.
- Name collisions cost about 6% of papers: GZMB "C11"/"CSPB", GSR "GluR", STRADA "LYK5",
  TNFSF10 "TRAIL" vs "TRAIL-R2".
- Generic binding entries (G1-G7) now link only when the paper used that entry's technique. This
  removed the CASP4 links to DSF, SPR, ITC and MS from one MST experiment.
- 7 of 20 targets gave nothing usable; CPB1 and SERPINA6 had no paper pass the fetch gate.
